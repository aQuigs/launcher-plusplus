# Privacy policy

Orbit Launcher (shown on the phone as Orbit) collects no personal data beyond the usage statistics below, which you can
turn off.

- Usage statistics: the app sends usage data to Google Analytics for Firebase so the developer can see how many people
  use it and on which devices. It covers app installs and opens, session counts and length, the app version, your device
  model, Android version, language and approximate country (worked out from your IP address, which is not stored). Each
  install is identified by a random ID that Firebase creates, not by your name, account or advertising ID. Nothing
  about your apps, notifications, layout or what you do on your phone is sent. Turn it off with "Share usage data" in
  the launcher's menu (long-press an empty spot on the home screen). Google processes this data under the
  [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- It lists the apps installed on your phone so it can show and open them. That list never leaves your phone.
- Notification access is optional. With it, the launcher counts each app's notifications to show unread badges. It never
  reads what a notification says.
- Usage access is optional. With it, the Most Used card ranks your apps by recent screen time. The launcher reads those
  figures from Android when it shows the card and does not store them.
- Do Not Disturb access is optional and only lets the ringer switch change your phone's ringer mode.
- Your home screen layout, collections, widgets and settings are stored on your phone, in the app's private storage.
- There are no ads, no advertising ID, no accounts and no third-party services other than Firebase Analytics.
- If your phone backs up app data to your own Google account (Android's standard backup), that copy is managed by Google
  under your account settings. The developer never receives it.
- Uninstalling the app, or resetting it from its menu, deletes everything it stored on your phone, the usage statistics'
  random ID included. Usage data already sent stays in Google Analytics until its retention period ends.
